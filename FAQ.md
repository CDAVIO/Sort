# SORT FAQ: photo organizer for Mac and Windows

**What is SORT?**
SORT is a desktop photo organizer for macOS and Windows. It sorts photos and videos into folders by camera, date and place, renames them, and finds duplicate photos. Everything runs on your computer.

**Is there a free version?**
Every new account gets 7 days of Copy and Move for free, with no card. Find duplicates and the full year are part of the yearly plan.

**How much does it cost?**
45 € per year, VAT included, for every Mac and PC you own. Nothing renews by itself. Refund within 14 days, from your account. Businesses with a valid EU VAT number can pay without VAT. [Pricing](https://sort.cdav.io/pricing).

**How do I pay?**
Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, through Stripe. SORT never sees your card.

**Are my photos uploaded?**
No. SORT reads your photos on your computer and uploads nothing. Your account only holds your plan and invoices. [Privacy Policy](https://sort.cdav.io/privacy).

**Can it lose a photo?**
Copy never touches the originals. Move removes an original only after its copy has been written and its size checked. Duplicates go to a folder or the Trash, never deleted for good. Keep a backup of anything you cannot replace.

**What is the difference between Copy and Move?**
Copy keeps your originals and makes sorted copies. Move puts the photos themselves into the folders and frees the space. [Copy vs Move](guides/copy-vs-move.md).

**How does it find duplicates?**
It compares the pictures themselves with a tiny fingerprint, so it finds exact copies and resized ones such as WhatsApp sends. No AI, no internet. [Find duplicate photos](guides/find-duplicate-photos.md).

**Does it work with iPhone photos (HEIC) and Live Photos?**
Yes. HEIC, HEIF and ProRAW DNG are read, and a Live Photo's picture and video stay in the same folder.

**Does it work with Android, Canon, Sony, Nikon, DJI and GoPro files?**
Yes. Any camera that writes its model into the file gets its own folder, raw files included.

**What happens to a photo with no date or no GPS?**
It simply skips that folder level instead of landing in a junk folder.

**How big a library can it handle?**
Large ones. 100 GB uses about as much memory as 100 MB, because scanning reads only each file's header.

**Which languages?**
