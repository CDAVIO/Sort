Build the folder structure

Start from a preset or click tokens to compose your own. If a file has no GPS or no date, that level simply disappears from its path.

Screenshots get their own folder
They skip the device level and go to Screenshots / Year / Month.
Keep Live Photo pairs together
IMG_0001.HEIC and IMG_0001.MOV land in the same folder.
Keep sidecar files with their photo
.AAE, .XMP, .THM and .LRV follow the file they belong to.
Handle non-media files too
Anything that isn't a photo or video goes to _Others. Off means SORT leaves them alone.

Rename as you go

Optional. Keep the camera's names, or rebuild them from the capture date.

Two files with the same new name? The second becomes _2, the third _3. Extensions are always preserved.

Choose where it goes

Pick a destination outside your source folder, then decide how careful you want to be.
